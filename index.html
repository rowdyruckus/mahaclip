MahaClip

Short clips from the weekly MAHA Action Media Hub.

MahaClip is a small publishing tool used by one creator. Each week it takes the public MAHA Action Media Hub livestream, finds the strongest moments, and turns them into captioned vertical shorts.

The creator reviews every short in the MahaClip app. When they greenlight one, MahaClip uploads that single video to the creator's own TikTok account through TikTok's official Content Posting API, with the caption, privacy setting and interaction settings the creator chose on the greenlight screen.

How MahaClip uses TikTok

  • The creator signs in with TikTok (Login Kit) once and grants permission to post on their behalf.
  • Nothing is posted without the creator pressing Greenlight on that specific video.
  • Before posting, the app shows the creator's TikTok account name, a privacy choice and TikTok's Music Usage Confirmation.
  • MahaClip does not read, follow, like, comment on or message anything on TikTok.
