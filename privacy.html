Privacy Policy

Last updated October 14, 2026

MahaClip is a personal publishing tool operated for a single creator. This policy explains what information MahaClip handles when that creator connects a TikTok account.

Information we receive from TikTok

  • An access token and refresh token, and the account's open ID, issued by TikTok when the creator authorizes MahaClip.
  • Basic creator information returned by TikTok's API (display name, username, avatar URL, allowed privacy options), used only to show the creator which account a video will be posted to.
  • The status of each post MahaClip submits (for example "processing" or "published").

How we use it

Only to upload videos the creator explicitly greenlights to the creator's own TikTok account, and to show the result of each upload. MahaClip does not collect information about other TikTok users, does not read feeds, comments or messages, and does not use TikTok data for advertising, profiling or analytics.

Storage and security

Tokens are stored on MahaClip's private server, readable only by the MahaClip service, and are transmitted only to TikTok's API over HTTPS. Videos posted are the creator's own clips.

Sharing

We do not sell, rent or share information received from TikTok with anyone. It is sent only to TikTok, as needed to complete a post.

Retention and deletion

Tokens are kept while the TikTok account stays connected. The creator can disconnect at any time from TikTok's settings (Manage app permissions), which revokes the tokens; MahaClip then deletes its stored copies. Deletion requests can also be made through the contact link below.

Children

MahaClip is not directed to children and is not used by anyone other than the creator who operates it.

Changes

Any change to this policy will be posted on this page with a new date.
